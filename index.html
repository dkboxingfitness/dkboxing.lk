About the Club
Blomendhal Warriors

Blomendhal Warriors Sports Club (BWSC) is an officially registered multi-sport club based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Whether you play cricket, swim competitively, play chess or box — you can represent your sport under the Blomendhal Warriors banner.

The club provides athletes across all disciplines a registered, recognised platform to participate in official competitions and events across Sri Lanka.

BASL Registered GN Registered Colombo 15

About the Brand
DK Boxing Fitness
Train Your Mind · Train Your Body · Unleash Your Power

DK Boxing Fitness is the active coaching brand of Blomendhal Warriors, founded by Dhanush Kumar. Based on a training terrace in Mattakuliya, Colombo 15, DK Boxing Fitness offers professional boxing classes for all levels.

From self-defense and fitness conditioning to preparing fighters for official BASL competitions — DK Boxing Fitness is where the real work happens.

BASL Registered Est. 2025 Colombo 15
What We Offer
Our Services
01
Self-Defense Boxing
Practical self-defense techniques through boxing fundamentals. Build confidence, awareness and the ability to protect yourself.
02
Fitness & Conditioning
High-intensity boxing drills for strength, endurance and full body conditioning. Push your limits every session.
03
Boxing for Weight Loss
A targeted boxing program combining cardio and strength training for effective, sustainable weight loss.
04
Competition & Tournaments
Train to fight. We prepare fighters for official BASL-registered competitions under the Blomendhal Warriors banner.
